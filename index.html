I’m interested in how things work — and how they can work better.

My approach to development starts with understanding the problem, mapping the workflow, and building something people can actually use. I’m especially drawn to web applications, internal tools, and systems that make everyday work simpler.

When I step away from the screen, I usually bring a camera. Cinematography and color grading give me another way to think about composition, detail, and the feeling behind a finished piece.
